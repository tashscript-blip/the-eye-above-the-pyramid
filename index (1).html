WHAT YOU WILL DISCOVER

Six chapters of ancient revelation, hidden history, and personal transformation.

I

Stones That Remember

The true builders of the pyramids: skilled African architects, mathematicians, and priests whose genius was systematically erased. Imhotep and the nameless thousands who encoded the cosmos in stone.

II

The Architecture of Heaven

Sacred geometry, stellar alignments to Orion's Belt and Sirius, and the profound mystery of the Great Pyramid's missing capstone. The pyramid as a map from earth to the divine.

III

The Eye That Watches

Tracing the Eye of Providence back to its true origin: the Eye of Horus in ancient Egypt. Why does the eye float above, separated from the pyramid — and what does that gap mean for you?

IV

The Mystery Schools

What was taught in the temples of Heliopolis and Memphis. The seven Hermetic Principles of Thoth, and how Pythagoras and Plato were students of African wisdom before that lineage was erased.

V

The Floating Eye: A Map for the Modern Soul

Kemetic philosophy, the 42 Laws of Ma'at, and the ancient soul model as a personal framework for elevation. The gap between the eye and the pyramid is sacred space — it is the place of becoming.

VI

Becoming the Capstone

The call to integration: honor your builders, close the gap, and live from your highest awareness. You are the unplaced capstone. It is time.

About the Author

TASH

TASH is a writer, thinker, and seeker based in Manhattan, New York — a city built on layers of hidden history, much like the pyramids he writes about. Driven by a lifelong conviction that the most important knowledge is the knowledge most deliberately concealed, TASH moves through the worlds of history, spirituality, and self-development with a rare fluency. He believes that ancient African and Egyptian wisdom is not a relic of the past but a living inheritance — one that modern people have every right to claim. The Eye Above the Pyramid is his debut book, born from years of study, reflection, and a burning need to give voice to the nameless builders whose legacy we are still living inside. TASH writes for those who know, deep in their bones, that they are built for more.
